# CurioQuest Phase 1 — Video Production Catalog

24 individual hero clips. Each ~10s. Individual files, never stitched.
Output: `~/workspace/imagine_media/phase1/<filename>.mp4`

## Style suffix (append to EVERY prompt)

Premium polished 3D children's educational cartoon, soft rounded shapes, warm soft lighting, gentle calm animation, clean composition with space for UI overlay, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

## Cast reference (describe in every prompt)

- **Curio the fox:** small orange fox kit, cream belly and tail tip, big amber eyes, friendly.
- **Nova:** young child explorer, ~6 years old, round face, short dark hair, teal explorer vest, tiny backpack, compass badge.
- **Luna the owl:** round lavender-purple owl, huge cream facial discs, tiny beak, big gentle amber eyes.
- **Milo the robot:** round white robot, dome head, springy antenna with glowing blue bulb, blue chest light, real dark friendly eyes.
- **Bea the bee:** round fuzzy yellow bee, curved black stripes, translucent fluttering wings, dot antennae, no stinger.
- **Tuno the turtle:** small green turtle, low dome shell with soft rounded patches, slow calm eyes.
- **Riff the rabbit:** tall joyful rabbit, long upright ears, warm gray fur, pink inner ears.
- **Atlas the elephant:** baby elephant, soft slate blue-gray, big floppy ears, short curling trunk.

## Formula (every prompt)

Character + subject + action + educational moment + interaction pause + mood + camera.

## BATCH 1 — Specialist intros (agent 1)

### 1. luna-intro.mp4
Luna the owl, a round lavender-purple owl with huge cream facial discs, tiny orange beak and big gentle amber eyes, reading teacher. She flutters down from a moonlit branch and lands beside a giant open storybook, wingtip tracing under a row of big floating letters that glow softly as she passes. Educational moment: she looks directly at the camera, tilts her head, and holds a warm inviting pause as if waiting for the child to say the letters with her. Mood: calm, bookish, encouraging. Camera: slow push-in from medium to close-up. STYLE.

### 2. milo-intro.mp4
Milo the robot, a round white robot with a dome head, springy antenna with glowing blue bulb, blue chest light and real dark friendly eyes, math and coding teacher. He rolls up to three big glowing number blocks showing 1, 2, 3, taps each one with a stubby arm so it lights up with a soft chime, then turns to the camera and waves. Educational moment: he points at the blocks, then at the viewer, holding a pause as if inviting the child to count along. Mood: bright, playful, clever. Camera: gentle arc from side to front. STYLE.

### 3. bea-intro.mp4
Bea the bee, a round fuzzy yellow bee with curved black stripes, translucent fluttering wings and dot antennae, no stinger, science teacher. She zips in a loop-the-loop and lands on a big sunflower, pollen sparkles drifting around her. Educational moment: she leans close to the flower's center, looks at the camera and wiggles her antennae, pausing as if asking the child what they notice. Mood: cheerful, curious, buzzy but calm. Camera: follows her flight then settles into a steady close-up. STYLE.

### 4. tuno-intro.mp4
Tuno the turtle, a small green turtle with a low dome shell with soft rounded patches and slow calm eyes, calm-down teacher. He sits on a smooth pond stone, takes one slow visible breath — shell gently rising and falling — then opens his eyes and gives the camera a slow warm blink. Educational moment: he holds the calm gaze, unhurried, giving the child time to settle. Mood: deeply calm, safe, unhurried. Camera: very slow push-in, almost still. STYLE.

### 5. riff-intro.mp4
Riff the rabbit, a tall joyful rabbit with long upright ears, warm gray fur and pink inner ears, music teacher. He hops into frame, claps his paws twice in a simple rhythm — clap, clap, pause — ears bouncing with each clap, then leans toward the camera grinning. Educational moment: he holds the pause after the rhythm, eyebrows raised, clearly waiting for the child to clap it back. Mood: joyful, rhythmic, inviting. Camera: steady medium shot, slight bounce with the rhythm. STYLE.

### 6. atlas-intro.mp4
Atlas the elephant, a baby elephant in soft slate blue-gray with big floppy ears and a short curling trunk, geography teacher. He stands beside a big slowly-spinning globe, touches a continent with the tip of his trunk, and the spot glows warmly. Educational moment: he looks from the globe to the camera with a curious raised brow, pausing as if asking "where shall we go?" Mood: kind, wonder-filled, grand but gentle. Camera: slow orbit around elephant and globe. STYLE.

## BATCH 2 — Teaching moments (agent 2)

### 7. luna-letter-trace.mp4
Luna the owl (round lavender-purple owl, huge cream facial discs, tiny beak), reading teacher. In a cozy moonlit library nook, she traces a giant glowing letter A in the air with her wingtip — down, down, across — the stroke lingering as light. Educational moment: she finishes, taps the letter, looks at the camera and holds a pause, inviting the child to say its sound. Mood: gentle, focused, magical. Camera: locked medium shot. STYLE.

### 8. milo-counting.mp4
Milo the robot (round white robot, dome head, antenna with blue bulb, real dark eyes), math teacher. Three shiny red apples appear one by one on a wooden table — Milo taps each as it appears: tap, tap, tap — then sweeps an arm across all three. Educational moment: he holds up three stubby fingers, looks at the camera, pausing for the child to say "three". Mood: bright, clear, encouraging. Camera: steady, slight push-in on the count. STYLE.

### 9. bea-growth.mp4
Bea the bee (round fuzzy yellow bee, black stripes, translucent wings), science teacher. A tiny seed in soil, time-lapse: sprout pushes up, two leaves unfurl, a bud opens into a flower — Bea watches from a nearby leaf, wings fluttering with excitement. Educational moment: she points a tiny arm at each stage, then looks at the camera, pausing as if asking what changed. Mood: wonder, gentle discovery. Camera: low angle, slow rise with the sprout. STYLE.

### 10. tuno-breathe.mp4
Tuno the turtle (small green turtle, dome shell with soft patches), calm teacher. Close-up: his shell slowly rises as he breathes in, holds, then softly falls as he breathes out — a faint glowing ring expands and contracts around him in sync. Educational moment: the breathing loop repeats twice, slow and steady, giving the child time to breathe along. Mood: deeply calming, safe. Camera: locked close-up, barely moving. STYLE.

### 11. riff-copy-rhythm.mp4
Riff the rabbit (tall, long upright ears, warm gray fur), music teacher. He stomps and claps a simple pattern — stomp, clap, clap — then freezes with paws up and a big grin. Educational moment: he holds the freeze, ears perked, clearly waiting for the child to copy the pattern back. Mood: playful, rhythmic. Camera: steady medium shot. STYLE.

### 12. atlas-continents.mp4
Atlas the elephant (baby elephant, slate blue-gray, big floppy ears, short trunk), geography teacher. A big globe spins slowly; his trunk tip touches three glowing spots one by one — each glows and plays a soft tone. Educational moment: he looks at the camera after the third, trunk raised, pausing as if asking which place the child likes. Mood: wonder, gentle grandeur. Camera: slow orbit. STYLE.

## BATCH 3 — Welcome, entry, transitions (agent 3)

### 13. curio-welcome.mp4
Curio the fox, a small orange fox kit with cream belly and tail tip and big amber eyes, quest guide. He trots into a sunny flower meadow, looks straight at the camera, waves a paw, and does a happy little spin — tail poofing out. Educational moment: he stops, tilts his head with a warm smile, holding an inviting pause as if saying "ready for an adventure?" Mood: warm, welcoming, exciting. Camera: low angle push-in. STYLE.

### 14. nova-entry.mp4
Nova, a young child explorer about 6 years old with a round face, short dark hair, teal explorer vest, tiny backpack and compass badge. She runs down a path into a magical learning world — glowing trees, floating sparkles — then stops, turns back to the camera, and beckons "come on!" Educational moment: she holds the beckon, smiling, waiting for the child to join. Mood: adventurous, friendly, peer-like. Camera: follows her run, settles as she turns. STYLE.

### 15. portal-forest.mp4
Curio the fox (small orange fox kit, cream tail tip) and Nova the child explorer (6 years old, teal vest, backpack) walk hand-in-paw toward a glowing archway of woven branches and fireflies; they step through and the light blooms into a lush forest learning world. Pure wonder, no dialogue needed. Mood: magical, inviting. Camera: follows them through the portal. STYLE.

### 16. portal-ocean.mp4
A sunny beach scene: gentle waves part like a curtain, revealing a glowing underwater world with coral and friendly fish; Bea the bee (round fuzzy yellow bee) hovers at the edge, looking back at the camera invitingly. Mood: wonder, gentle excitement. Camera: slow glide from beach into the water world. STYLE.

### 17. portal-space.mp4
Night sky: stars swirl into a glowing spiral portal; Milo the robot (round white robot, antenna with blue bulb) rides a small round rocket through it, waving, into a colorful planetarium world with friendly planets. Mood: awe, playful. Camera: follows the rocket through the swirl. STYLE.

### 18. meadow-hub.mp4
Establishing shot: a sunny home meadow hub — round hill, big friendly tree with a treehouse, flower paths leading to three glowing portals (forest, ocean, space). Curio the fox sits by the treehouse waving. Calm, no fast motion. Mood: safe, home, inviting. Camera: slow aerial drift. STYLE.

Curio the fox (small orange fox kit, cream tail tip, big amber eyes) jumps with joy, tail wagging, as golden stars burst around him; he catches one star and holds it up to the camera proudly. Mood: triumphant, warm. Camera: slight low angle, energetic but not shaky. STYLE.

### 20. cast-finale.mp4
All eight friends dance together in the sunny meadow: Curio the fox, Nova the child explorer, Luna the owl, Milo the robot, Bea the bee, Tuno the turtle, Riff the rabbit, Atlas the elephant — a joyful simple dance, confetti and sparkles falling. They all turn to the camera at the end, waving, holding a happy pause. Mood: pure celebration, togetherness. Camera: wide shot, slow push-in. STYLE.

### 21. nova-highfive.mp4
Nova the child explorer (6 years old, teal vest, backpack) runs up to the camera, grinning, and holds up her hand for a high-five — holding the pose, waiting. Mood: peer joy, playful. Camera: steady, at child's eye level. STYLE.

Luna the owl (round lavender owl, huge facial discs) does a happy little wing-flap dance on her branch, feathers bouncing, then leans toward the camera with a proud warm look. Mood: proud, gentle. Camera: medium shot. STYLE.

### 23. milo-dance.mp4
Milo the robot (round white robot, antenna with blue bulb) does a happy robot dance — side to side, antenna bulb flashing in rhythm, chest light pulsing. Mood: silly, joyful. Camera: steady medium shot. STYLE.

### 24. tuno-proud.mp4
Tuno the turtle (small green turtle, dome shell) gives a slow happy nod, closes his eyes in a contented smile, a soft golden glow around him. Calm celebration — no jumping. Mood: warm, peaceful pride. Camera: slow push-in. STYLE.
