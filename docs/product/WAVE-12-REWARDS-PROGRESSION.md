# Wave 12 — Rewards, Achievements, and World Progression

## Goal

Create motivating progression that reinforces learning rather than screen time.

## Reward Types

Possible systems:

- XP
- stars
- badges
- achievements
- trophies
- collectibles
- avatar items
- world unlocks
- streaks

## Reward Meaningful Behavior

Reward:

- lesson completion
- skill mastery
- persistence
- successful review
- reading consistency
- creative work
- difficult challenges
- improvement

Do not reward meaningless clicking.

## World Progression

CurioQuest may use themed learning regions such as:

- Reading Forest
- Number Mountain
- Science Lab
- Puzzle Planet
- Creative Studio
- Story World
- Discovery Museum
- Space Explorer
- Nature World

Use only themes consistent with the current brand.

## Age Adaptation

Younger children may prefer:

- visual unlocks
- simple stars
- collectibles

Older children may prefer:

- goals
- achievement collections
- challenge progress
- world completion

Avoid manipulative dark patterns.

## Completion Criteria

- reward logic is data-driven
- rewards relate to real learning
- progression is visible but not distracting
- production build passes
