# Wave 11 — Stories, Video, Audio, and Discovery

## Goal

Create rich media experiences that support active learning rather than passive consumption.

## Story Library

Support:

- picture stories
- read-to-me
- read-yourself
- decodable readers
- leveled stories
- informational reading
- comprehension
- vocabulary
- story sequencing

## Age Adaptation

Younger:

- shorter stories
- strong illustrations
- read-to-me
- simple interaction

Older:

- longer passages
- informational reading
- vocabulary
- deeper comprehension
- inference

## Audio

Treat audio as first-class.

Support:

- spoken instructions
- phoneme/letter sound playback
- word pronunciation
- story narration
- replay controls

Do not rely on browser speech synthesis as the only long-term solution.

## Video

Educational videos should connect to active learning.

Prefer flows such as:

Watch
→ Predict
→ Answer
→ Apply/Create

Avoid building a passive endless-video library.

## Discovery

Create exploratory experiences that may combine:

- geography
- animals
- cultures
- inventions
- transportation
- space
- nature
- community
- technology

## Performance

Use efficient media loading.

Do not preload large media libraries.

## Completion Criteria

- story library structure
- media-responsive layout
- fullscreen support
- age-specific stories/discovery
- production build passes
